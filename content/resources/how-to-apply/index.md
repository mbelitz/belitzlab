        **We are not actively recruiting new graduate students or postdocs this year.** That said, we're always happy to hear from prospective lab members at any career stage. Feel free to reach out below and we'll keep your information on file for future openings.

        ### M.S. students
        1. Read through our [Research](../../research/) page and our [publications on Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2QnS7eQAAAAJ&hl=en) to see if your interests overlap with the lab's.
        2. Email Michael a brief introduction: your research interests, relevant experience, and what draws you to the lab. Attach a CV.
        3. If there's a good fit and potential for available funding, we'll set up a call to talk further.
        4. Apply to the [UW-Madison Department of Entomology graduate program](https://entomology.wisc.edu/graduate-program/) by the relevant deadline, and note your interest in the lab in your application materials.

        ### Ph.D. students
        1. Read through our [Research](../../research/) page and our [publications on Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2QnS7eQAAAAJ&hl=en) to see if your interests overlap with the lab's.
        2. Email Michael a brief introduction: your research interests, relevant experience, and what draws you to the lab. Attach a CV.
        3. If there's a good fit and potential for available funding, we'll set up a call to talk further.
        4. Apply through one of two graduate programs, depending on fit: the [UW-Madison Department of Entomology graduate program](https://entomology.wisc.edu/graduate-program/), or the [Ecology & Evolutionary Biology (EEB) PhD program](https://cals.wisc.edu/academics/graduate/ecologyevolutionarybiophd/). Note your interest in the lab in your application materials.

        ### Postdoctoral researchers
        Reach out by email with a CV and a short description of your research interests and career goals. If you're interested in applying for your own fellowship (e.g., NSF PRFB, USDA NIFA postdoctoral fellowship, Smith Fellowship), I'm glad to discuss potential projects and provide mentorship on the application well before the relevant deadlines.

        ### Undergraduate researchers
        UW-Madison undergraduates can reach out directly by email with a short note about your interests and availability. No prior research experience is necessary.
