        ### Graduate students
        1. Read through our [Research](../../research/) and [Publications](../../publication/) pages to see if your interests overlap with the lab's.
        2. Email Michael a brief introduction: your research interests, relevant experience, and what draws you to the lab. Attach a CV and unofficial transcript.
        3. If there's a good fit, we'll set up a call to talk further before you commit time to a formal application.
        4. Apply to the [UW-Madison Department of Entomology graduate program](https://entomology.wisc.edu/graduate-program/) by the relevant deadline, and note your interest in the lab in your application materials.

        ### Postdoctoral researchers
        Reach out by email with a CV and a short description of your research interests and career goals. If you're interested in applying for your own fellowship (e.g., NSF PRFB, NSF PRFB-BIO, USDA NIFA postdoctoral fellowship), I'm glad to discuss potential projects and provide mentorship on the application well before the relevant deadlines.

        ### Undergraduate researchers
        UW-Madison undergraduates can reach out directly by email with a short note about your interests and availability. No prior research experience is necessary.
