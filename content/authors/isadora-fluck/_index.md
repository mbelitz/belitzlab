Isadora E. Fluck is a postdoctoral research associate who studies how biodiversity is organized and how it changes across space, species, and environments. Her research combines large-scale ecological datasets, computational approaches, and ecological theory to uncover patterns in species composition, trait variation, and community structure.

She received her Ph.D. in Interdisciplinary Ecology from the University of Florida.
