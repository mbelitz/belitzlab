        We are actively recruiting graduate students and postdoctoral researchers to join the lab at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. We are building a diverse, collaborative team unified by an interest in using data science and field approaches to understand how insects respond to global change.

        ### Prospective Ph.D. students
        Applicants should apply directly to the [Department of Entomology graduate program](https://entomology.wisc.edu/graduate-program/) at UW-Madison. Before applying, please reach out with a brief email describing your research interests, relevant experience, and why you're interested in the lab — attach a CV and unofficial transcript if possible. I try to respond to every inquiry, though it may take a few weeks during busy periods.

        ### Prospective postdocs
        I'm happy to discuss postdoctoral fellowship opportunities (e.g., NSF PRFB, USDA NIFA) as well as openings tied to funded grants as they become available. Reach out with a CV and a short description of your research interests and how they connect to the lab's work.

        ### Prospective undergraduate researchers
        UW-Madison undergraduates interested in gaining research experience in ecology, data science, or entomology are welcome to reach out. No prior research experience is required — interest and reliability matter most.

        ### Our commitment
        I actively participate in and support programs aimed at increasing diversity in science, and I'm committed to fostering an inclusive, welcoming lab environment where people from all backgrounds can do their best work. See our [Resources](../resources/) page for our lab code of conduct and other guiding documents.
