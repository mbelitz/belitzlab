We're excited to be launching the Belitz Global Change Entomology Lab in the Department of Entomology at the University of Wisconsin-Madison in August 2026. The lab studies how insects and ecosystems respond to climate change, urbanization, and agricultural intensification, combining data science and field approaches to inform conservation and climate adaptation.

We are actively recruiting graduate students and postdoctoral researchers — see our [Join Us](../../join/) page for more information.
